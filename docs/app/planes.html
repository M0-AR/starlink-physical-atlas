04 / Planes

Structure behind the points.

A TLE snapshot spans days, and RAAN precesses under Earth’s equatorial bulge. Without rectification, the plane lattice smears by up to ~18°. The atlas removes that predictable clock before counting effective planes.

The key distinction

Measured lattice ≠ Walker design.

An effective plane is a cluster of similar RAAN. Merged or partially filled planes change the measured lattice. The atlas reports what the public data shows, not what a clean design document might specify.

The 97.4° shell is the clearest warning: 45 tight groups, 72.6% tight fraction, and a spacing CV of 1.62. Its effective count is real, but a simple “planes × satellites per plane” reading would be misleading.

J2 check: the median absolute relative error between measured nodal drift and the J2 prediction is 0.066%; p95 is 0.47%; maximum is 0.915%.
